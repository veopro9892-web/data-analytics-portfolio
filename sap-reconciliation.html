Technical Challenge: The Cartesian Join Fan-Out Trap & Solution

Test case PO-45001003 has 2 split Goods Receipts (40 + 60 = 100 units) and 2 split Invoices (40 + 60 @ ₹1000 = ₹100,000).

CORRECT ARCHITECTURE (INDEPENDENT CTE AGGREGATION)
ZERO ARTIFICIAL INFLATION
Joined Output Rows 1 Row Exact 1-to-1 PO Line
Reported Received Qty 100.0 Units Agrees with delivery dock
Reported Invoiced Qty 100.0 Units Matches vendor bill
Reported Invoiced Amount ₹1,00,000.00 100% Accurate

Solution Architecture: Receipts and Invoices are pre-aggregated in separate CTEs at the (po_number, line_item) level before performing a LEFT JOIN to the purchase order line. This isolates split deliveries and split progress billings, completely preventing row duplication.
