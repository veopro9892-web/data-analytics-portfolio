Campaign Architecture

  • Target Time Window: Active exclusively between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM (Monday through Friday).
  • Bundle Offering: 1x Vanilla Cold Brew (₹286 delivery menu price) + 1x Butter Flaky Croissant (₹198 delivery menu price). Standalone total = ₹484.
  • Special Combo Price: ₹399 (17.5% bundle incentive).
  • Placement: Featured carousel banner on Zomato Merchant App targeted at office delivery addresses within 3 km.
